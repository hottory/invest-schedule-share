Privacy Policy

TechxMoney Uploader — Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what TechxMoney Uploader (“the Service”, “we”) collects when you use it to publish videos to TikTok, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to publish a video to TikTok on your behalf:

  • TikTok account information — the account identifier and display name returned by TikTok when you authorize the Service, and the privacy and publishing settings available for that account.
  • Video and caption data — the vertical video file you upload and the caption, privacy level, and disclosure settings you choose for that post.
  • OAuth tokens — the access token and refresh token TikTok issues after you authorize the Service. These tokens are what allow the Service to act on your behalf.
  • Posting records — the result of a publish or upload request (for example, whether it succeeded and any error returned by TikTok), kept so we can diagnose failed posts.

We do not collect your TikTok password. We do not collect your contacts, location, or browsing history, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used only to provide the Service you asked for:

  • to connect to the TikTok account you authorized;
  • to upload and publish the video and caption you supplied, with the settings you chose;
  • to refresh an expired access token so a post you requested can proceed; and
  • to diagnose a failed upload or publish request.

We do not use your videos, captions, or account information for any other purpose, and we do not use them to train models or to target advertising.

3. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored securely and are used only for the publishing requests you authorize. Video files are handled only for the duration of the upload; we do not keep a separate library of your videos beyond what is needed to complete a post and confirm its result. Posting records are kept only as long as needed to diagnose issues and are then deleted.

If you revoke the Service’s access from your TikTok settings, the tokens we hold stop working. You may also ask us to delete the tokens and posting records we hold for you — see Section 6.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The only external service your data is sent to is TikTok itself, because publishing to TikTok is the Service you requested. TikTok’s handling of that data is governed by TikTok’s own Privacy Policy.

We will disclose information only if required by law or to protect the security of the Service.

5. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • revoke the Service’s access from your TikTok account settings;
  • ask what posting records we hold for your account; and
  • ask us to delete the tokens and posting records we hold for you.

Deleting a post from TikTok must be done in the TikTok app. The Service cannot delete a video after it has been published.

6. Contact and deletion requests

For questions about this Policy or to request deletion of the data we hold for you, please contact us through the TechxMoney blog: https://blog.naver.com/yeonwoomyung. Please include your TikTok username so we can identify the correct account.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this Policy

If this Policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be noted on the Service’s main page.
