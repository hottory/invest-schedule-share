TechxMoney Uploader

Publish short news videos to TikTok

A web tool for creators to publish short AI and semiconductor news videos to TikTok with captions and privacy controls.

TechxMoney Uploader is built for creators who produce short daily news briefings — in particular AI and semiconductor news — and want a reliable, reviewable way to post those videos to TikTok. The tool is operated by TechxMoney, whose TikTok account is @techxmoney_official.

This app is currently under review by TikTok. It is not a scheduling service: posts are published immediately or saved as a draft for the creator to finish in the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account. You sign in with TikTok and authorize TechxMoney Uploader to publish on your behalf. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings.
  2. Upload a vertical video. Upload a short vertical video (MP4) prepared for TikTok.
  3. Add a caption and choose settings. Add a caption, choose the privacy level, and set commercial-content disclosure where it applies.
  4. Publish or save as a draft. Publish the video directly, or send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft and finish it yourself in the TikTok app.
Before TikTok’s Content Posting audit is complete, videos published through the API are visible only to the creator (SELF_ONLY / private). Public posting becomes available only after the audit is passed. Drafts are not published by the app at all — the creator publishes them from the TikTok app.

The app does not schedule posts for a future time, does not post on behalf of accounts that have not authorized it, and does not edit or re-publish a video after it has been published.

Who it is for

TechxMoney Uploader is for creators who make their own short news videos and want to publish them to their own TikTok accounts. It is not a tool for managing other people’s accounts, and it is not for posting content you do not own or do not have the right to publish.

You are responsible for the videos and captions you publish, for respecting copyright and TikTok’s Community Guidelines, and for disclosing branded or paid content where required.
